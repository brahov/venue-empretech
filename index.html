05 · Why this is not a booking tool

What we saw in Mykonos, and what is missing

In September we booked at six venues in Mykonos in one week. Every venue handled the paperwork. None used what it knew about the guest.

Booking platform

The paperwork, automatically

  • Confirmation, deposit, cancellation rule
  • Sends a reminder, sometimes asks to reconfirm
  • Does not know who the guest is
By hand

A person, when someone is free

  • A shared email box or a central desk
  • Uses nothing the venue knows about the guest
The booking agent

The next step, on top of your systems

  • Answers at the hour the guest asks
  • Knows the guest across all venues
  • Offers, confirms and follows up within your rules
  • Works with the systems you already use
